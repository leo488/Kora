Motion

New

Thirteen motion components, each running live here with its spec. The rule across all of them: motion explains a change of state (money moved, a sheet appeared, a value updated); it never decorates. Durations stay between 90 and 480ms. Settings → Reduce motion swaps all of them for a 150ms fade.

M1

Pixel avatar builds

TriggerFirst Home load after sign-up; Profile open
TimingEach pixel 220ms, staggered 45ms along the diagonal. Under 900ms total
Easingcubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)
WhyIntroduces the user's identity once; never replays on every visit
